# HEARTHGLOAM

A sound instrument for tabletop role-playing games. One file, no install, no internet needed. Play monster voices live, run weather and ambience, a score that composes as you play, a doom clock, timed breaks with a pond you can play, and a game master's book. Everything you hear is made on the spot: no recorded samples.

## Use it
1. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser (works on phones). Or open the hosted page if one is listed here.
2. Tap to start, then play. A guide is built in.

Free at home, at clubs, and at charity events. Please credit it and tag it. Using it at an event where money is earned needs an annual event licence. Art creators can sell their own art packs freely. Full terms in [LICENSE].

## Add your own pictures
- Monsters: compass menu, TABLE, ART: PORTRAIT (pick one or many).
- Scenes: compass menu, TABLE, ART: LANDSCAPE.
- Characters: compass menu, WORLD, PARTY, ADD PICTURES.
- Or place `1.jpg` to `25.jpg` (monsters) and `1-1.jpg` to `1-25.jpg` (scenes) in the same folder as `index.html`.

Your pictures and notes stay on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
